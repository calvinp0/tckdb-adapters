# A1 — TCKDB demand surface

Companion narrative for `TCKDB_DEMAND.yml`.

**Scope:** `/home/calvin/code/TCKDB_v2`, working tree HEAD (`09bf0165`),
`tckdb-schemas` **0.22.0**. Authority is the Pydantic models under
`schemas/python/tckdb-schemas/tckdb_schemas/`. The stale
`schemas/python/tckdb-schemas/build/` tree was never read.

**Result:** 2085 rows.

| Root | Rows | required | optional | conditional |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `species_upload` | 830 | 192 | 493 | 145 |
| `reaction_upload` | 1255 | 337 | 674 | 244 |
| **total** | **2085** | **529** | **1167** | **389** |

`on_absence` buckets: 799 rows hard-reject, 22 rows produce a named
`UploadWarning`, 1264 rows are silently accepted. 162 rows carry a unit, 217
carry an enum domain, 221 cite an ADR or DR.

## Method

The inventory was generated by walking `ComputedSpeciesUploadRequest` and
`ComputedReactionUploadRequest` with `pydantic.BaseModel.model_fields`, so no
branch of the tree could be forgotten or silently truncated. Every generated row
was then overlaid with a hand-curated entry keyed by
`<DeclaringClass>.<field>` — that overlay is where `requiredness: conditional`,
`condition`, `on_absence`, `semantics` and `adr_ref` come from, and every entry
in it was written after reading the validator body, the workflow seam, or the
warning constructor it describes. Nothing in those four columns was inferred
from a type annotation.

`source` line numbers were computed by locating the field's declaration inside
the source of the class that actually declares it (walking the MRO), then
verified mechanically: all 2085 citations were re-opened and checked to contain
the field name, with the only 24 exceptions being the discriminated-union
variant rows (`…runtime{described}` etc.), which correctly cite the variant
class's `class` line.

The calculation sub-tree dominates the row count. It appears **six times** —
twice in `species_upload` (`primary_calculation`, `additional_calculations[]`)
and four times in `reaction_upload` (`species[].conformers[].calculation`,
`species[].calculations[]`, `transition_state.calculation`,
`transition_state.calculations[]`). Each instance is ~240 rows once
`execution_environment`, `scan_result`, `irc_result` and `path_search_result` are
expanded. FIELD_KEY rule 5 requires all six routes to be enumerated separately,
and they genuinely differ: `CalculationInBundle` (species root) carries typed
`opt_result`/`freq_result`/`sp_result` blocks and a structured `literature`,
whereas `ComputedReactionCalculationIn` (reaction root) inherits the *flat*
`opt_*`/`freq_*`/`sp_*` scalars from `shared.calculation_in.CalculationIn` plus a
`geometry_key` and a bare integer `literature_id`. That asymmetry is the single
largest source of adapter risk in the whole contract.

## Where the scientific weight sits

Ranked by how much a downstream consumer loses when the field is absent:

1. **The frequency block.** `freq_result.modes[]` /
   `freq_frequencies_cm1` is the richest thing a producer can deposit. ADR 0012
   made the complete signed frequency list load-bearing: a transition state with
   more than one imaginary mode is *accepted* only if it names its reaction
   coordinate (`reaction_coordinate_mode_index`) and declares what every other
   imaginary mode is (`imaginary_disposition` /
   `freq_imaginary_dispositions`). Omitting the list on such a record produces
   `transition_state_extra_imaginary_modes_not_assessable` **plus a structural
   flag** that excludes the record from default queries and bulk TS exports.
   Omitting the designation is a hard 422.
2. **`atom_map` (ADR 0011).** TCKDB never derives it. Absent → warning
   `reaction_atom_map_absent`; the reaction is then unusable for bond
   break/form read-back, reaction-path degeneracy derivation, atom-resolved KIE,
   and atom-mapped ML export. Indices are 1-based on **both** sides and are
   geometry-relative — every participant names the geometry its indices count
   into, and the map names the TS geometry even though there is only one it
   could be.
3. **`transition_state.validation_evidence[]`.** Structured IRC proof that the
   saddle point connects the declared endpoints. Absent (or present with no
   `passed=true` record) → `transition_state_missing_irc_evidence`. What is
   never accepted is *incomplete* evidence presented as passing: a passing
   record carrying participant mappings must cover every TS atom exactly once
   on both sides.
4. **`calculation.parameters[]`.** Looks like bookkeeping; it is not. Three
   canonical keys — `freq.hessian_method`, `grid.quality`, `opt.convergence` —
   resolve ADR 0012's noise floor τ. With no parameters, τ falls back to the
   conservative `protocol_not_recorded` value of 50 cm⁻¹ and borderline extra
   imaginary modes are judged more harshly than the calculation deserves.
5. **`calculation.artifacts[]`.** Attaching files is not just archival: an
   `input` artifact is parsed into parameter rows, an `output_log` reconciles
   the single-point energy (`sp_energy_filled_from_log` /
   `sp_energy_payload_log_mismatch`) *and* the declared charge/multiplicity
   against the log, and in the species bundle a `.fchk`/`.hess` artifact is
   parsed into the Hessian matrix. Not attaching them loses all four
   server-side enrichments silently.
6. **`hessian` (DR-0030).** The primitive behind frequencies, modes and
   thermochemistry. Its length is checked exactly against the bound geometry:
   `3N(3N+1)/2` packed lower-triangle entries in hartree/bohr².
7. **`statmech`.** Symmetry, optical isomers, rotational constants (recorded in
   *source order*, never re-sorted), and the frequency scale factor. The
   validator docstring names ARC explicitly and deliberately does **not**
   require `statmech_treatment`: "an absent field is honest where an invented
   one would not be". What it *does* require is that a rotor-aware treatment
   lists its torsions.
8. **`input_geometries` / `output_geometries`.** The fallback is narrower than
   it looks: with `output_geometries` empty, only `opt` gets a fallback
   `(role=final, output_order=1)` row — freq, sp, irc, scan and path_search get
   **zero** output-geometry rows.

## Things worth flagging to the other agents

- **Two warnings that a reader might expect and that do *not* fire on these
  routes.** `missing_software_release_provenance`,
  `missing_workflow_tool_provenance`, `missing_literature_provenance`,
  `missing_frequency_scale_factor_provenance` and
  `missing_level_of_theory_provenance` all exist in
  `provenance_warnings.py`, but the collectors that emit them
  (`collect_thermo_provenance_warnings`, `collect_statmech_provenance_warnings`,
  `collect_kinetics_provenance_warnings`) are only wired to the *standalone*
  thermo/statmech/kinetics routes. `POST /uploads/computed-species` and
  `POST /uploads/computed-reaction` call only `reconcile_species_entry` +
  `stationary_point_warnings` (+ workflow-produced warnings). So an omitted
  `workflow_tool_release` on a bundle is **completely silent**. The `on_absence`
  column says so explicitly for each of those rows.
- Likewise `freq_parser_extracted_but_modes_missing` only fires through
  `reconcile_species_entry_full`, which the conformer route uses and neither
  bundle route does.
- **Index base is not uniform.** `IRCPointPayload.point_index` and
  `PathSearchPointPayload.point_index` are **0-based**;
  `CalculationScanPointPayload.point_index`, every atom index, every
  `mode_index`, `constraint_index`, `coordinate_index`, `torsion_index` and
  `participant_index` are **1-based**. The atom map and IRC evidence mappings are
  1-based on both sides.
- **`ThermoInBundle` and `BundleThermoIn` are not the same shape.** The species
  bundle's thermo enforces `validate_has_scientific_content` (at least one of
  h298 / s298 / nasa / points) and unique `temperature_k`; the reaction bundle's
  per-species thermo enforces neither, and also lacks
  `h298_uncertainty_kj_mol`, `s298_uncertainty_j_mol_k`, `literature`,
  `software_release`, `workflow_tool_release`, `source_calculations` and
  `applied_energy_corrections`.
- **`StatmechInBundle` carries the three rotational constants;
  `BundleStatmechIn` does not.** A reaction upload therefore cannot deposit
  rotational constants at all, which also means its statmech can never trip the
  `has_rotational_structure` signal from constants alone (only from torsions).
- **`scf_stability` is unreachable from either bundle.**
  `SCFStabilityPayload` exists and is a field of the primitive
  `CalculationWithResultsPayload`, but neither `CalculationInBundle` nor
  `CalculationIn` declares it — and both models are `extra="forbid"`, so
  supplying it is a 422. It is enumerated in the YAML **only** where it is
  actually reachable (nowhere in these two roots), i.e. it is deliberately
  absent from the inventory. Flagging it here because it is exactly the kind of
  thing an adapter might try to send.
- **Two integer database ids leak into the wire contract.**
  `CalculationIn.literature_id` (reaction root only) and
  `SCFStabilityPayload.source_calculation_id` / `source_artifact_id`. Both are
  unusable from an external producer, and `literature_id` is the one hole in
  DR-0029's "no FK ids anywhere" rule — the species bundle correctly uses a
  structured `literature` object instead.
- `parameters_json` is walked recursively and hard-rejects six specific FK-id
  key names (`existing_calculation_id`, `existing_conformer_id`,
  `existing_conformer_observation_id`, `existing_species_entry_id`,
  `source_calculation_id`, `source_conformer_observation_id`) at any depth. It
  also validates a `tckdb_origin` sub-block against `CalculationOriginMetadata`
  (DR-0026) when present.
- `BundleKineticsIn` accepts only `arrhenius` and `modified_arrhenius`. All six
  other `KineticsModelKind` members are hard-rejected with a message pointing at
  the standalone kinetics endpoint.
- `reaction_family` is matched case- and punctuation-insensitively against 132
  canonical RMG families in `reaction_family.py`; a non-canonical label is
  accepted only with `reaction_family_source_note`.

## Deliberate exclusions

- **`parameters_json` contents.** One leaf row per FIELD_KEY rule 3. The
  `tckdb_origin` sub-block *is* schema-validated by
  `CalculationOriginMetadata`, but it lives inside an opaque `dict` and has no
  field path of its own, so it is described in that row's `semantics` rather
  than given invented paths.
- **Response models.** `ComputedSpeciesUploadResult`,
  `ComputedReactionUploadResult`, `ConformerUploadRefInBundle`,
  `CalculationUploadRefInBundle`, `ThermoUploadRefInBundle`,
  `StatmechUploadRefInBundle` and `UploadWarning` are what TCKDB *returns*, not
  what it accepts. Out of scope for a demand inventory.
- **Unreachable models in the same packages.** `CalculationPayload`,
  `CalculationWithResultsPayload`, `SCFStabilityPayload`,
  `ThermoNASA9IntervalCreate`, `ThermoWilhoitCreate`, `SoftwareRef` (except
  where reached through `FreqScaleFactorRef.software`),
  `CalculationScanCoordinateCreate` outside `scan_result`, and every
  network-PDep model. None of them is reachable from these two roots; a row for
  them would be a demand that does not exist.
- **`ComputedSpeciesUploadRequest.stationary_point_findings()` and siblings.**
  Methods, not fields.
- **Enums declared in `enums.py` but never reachable from these two roots**
  (`NetworkChannelKind`, `NetworkSolveCalculationRole`,
  `NetworkKineticsModelKind`, `KineticsDirection`, `EnergyZeroConvention`,
  `EnergyCorrectionConvention`, `TemperatureUnit`, `PressureUnit`,
  `HessianMethod`). `HessianMethod` deserves a note: it never appears as a field
  type, but its *values* are the legal `canonical_value`s for the
  `freq.hessian_method` parameter observation that resolves τ.

## Coverage self-assessment

**Structural completeness: high confidence.** The walk is mechanical over
`model_fields`, recursion-guarded, and every path is unique (2085 rows, 2085
distinct paths). There is no place a field could have been dropped by fatigue.
I re-derived and machine-checked all 2085 `file:line` citations.

**`requiredness` and `condition`: high confidence.** I read every
`@model_validator` and `@field_validator` in the reachable tree (124 decorator
sites across the two workflow modules, `fragments/`, `shared/calculation_in.py`,
`literature.py`, `energy_correction.py`, `thermo.py` and `statmech_bits.py`),
plus `stationary_point.py`, `reaction_atom_map.validate_reaction_atom_map` and
`ts_validation_evidence.validate_ts_evidence_set`. 389 rows are marked
`conditional` and all 389 carry a `condition`.

**`on_absence`: good confidence at the schema layer, moderate at the workflow
layer.** The 799 hard-reject rows follow directly from `is_required()` plus a
read validator. The 22 warning rows were each traced to a named code and its
constructor. The 1264 "silent" rows are the weaker claim: I verified that
neither bundle route runs the provenance collectors, but I did **not**
exhaustively trace every persistence function in
`backend/app/workflows/computed_species.py` (940 lines) and
`computed_reaction.py` (1090 lines) for warnings appended deeper in the call
graph. If a warning is emitted somewhere I did not look, it would show up as a
row I have marked "silently accepted" that in fact warns — which under-reports
TCKDB's demand rather than inventing it, so the bias is in the safe direction
for this audit.

**`semantics`: good confidence.** Every row has one, and 0 rows are
`UNVERIFIED`. Roughly 400 are hand-written from validator bodies and module
docstrings; the rest are taken verbatim from the model's own `:param:`
docstring or `Field(description=...)`. Where a field had neither, I wrote the
semantics from the surrounding code rather than leaving it blank — those are
the ones most worth a second reader, particularly the `note`/`notes` free-text
fields, where "free-text annotation" is a true but shallow statement.

**`units`: high confidence where present, and the absences are honest.** 162
rows carry a unit read verbatim off the field name. Three fields are genuinely
unitless in the schema and I said so rather than guessing:
`IRCPointPayload.reaction_coordinate` and
`PathSearchPointPayload.path_coordinate` (software-specific and unstated), and
`CalculationScanCoordinatePayload.step_size`/`start_value`/`end_value` (whose
unit is carried by the sibling `value_unit`, and is `null` when that is
omitted). `BundleKineticsIn.a`, `reported_ea`, `d_reported_ea` and
`a_uncertainty` are recorded as "declared by <sibling>_units" rather than being
normalised, per the FIELD_KEY convention.

**Known soft spots, in order of how much they would matter:**

1. The workflow-layer `on_absence` tracing described above.
2. `CalculationType.conf` is a legal `type` value with no result block and no
   validator mentioning it. I could not establish what a `conf`-type calculation
   is *for* from the schemas package alone; the enum member is listed in the
   `type` rows' `enum_values` without further comment.
3. `IRCPointPayload.reaction_coordinate` — I could not establish the unit or
   sign convention. No docstring, no validator, no DR text found.
4. The `species_upload` conformer-grouping behaviour (which observations land in
   the same `conformer_group`) is a service-layer decision (DR-0005) invisible
   from the payload. It affects what a producer should send as separate
   conformer entries but is not a field, so no row asserts anything about it.
